## KEY MECHANICS

### Stage 1: Roaming Auto-Advance
- **Timer**: 6 seconds per position
- **Hit Effect**: Immediately advances + restarts timer
- **Reset**: After 15 seconds post-hit, shots reset (can be reused)
- **Visual**: White strobe on GI + aqua flashing on active shot

### Stage 2: One-Shot Jackpot Lighting
- **Mechanic**: Hit ANY ramp/orbit → Super Jackpot lights
- **Shots Enabled**: Only 5 (Center Ramp, Left Orbit, Right Orbit, Inner Loop L/R)
- **No Progression**: Unlike Stage 1, doesn't require multiple shots
- **Visual**: Aqua flashing until lit, then bright aqua

### Stage 3: Shot Cycling
- **Reset Pattern**: Hit shot → that shot unlit, all others relit
- **Progressive**: Counts up (1/5, 2/5, 3/5, 4/5, 5/5)
- **Reusable**: Same shot can be hit multiple times
- **Complexity**: Full 9-shot playfield, player must choose next shot
